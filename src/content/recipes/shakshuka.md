## Ingredients

- 1 onion and 1 red pepper
- 400 g crushed tomatoes
- Cumin, pimentón and a pinch of chilli
- 4 eggs
- Coriander or parsley, and bread for dipping

## Steps

1. Soften the onion and pepper, then add the spices.
2. Add the tomato and let it reduce for 10 minutes.
3. Make little wells, crack in the eggs and cover until the whites set.
4. Finish with fresh herbs.
