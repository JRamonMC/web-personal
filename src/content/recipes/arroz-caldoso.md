## Ingredients

- 320 g round rice
- 1 red pepper, 1 grated tomato, 2 garlic cloves
- Seasonal artichokes or green beans
- 1 tsp pimentón de Murcia
- 1.5 l vegetable stock

## Steps

1. Fry the pepper and vegetables in olive oil.
2. Add the garlic and tomato and reduce well. Off the heat, stir in the pimentón.
3. Pour in the hot stock, add the rice and simmer for about 18 minutes.
4. Rest for 2 minutes and serve straight away.
