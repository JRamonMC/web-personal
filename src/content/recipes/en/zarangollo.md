## Ingredients

- 3 medium courgettes
- 2 onions
- 4 eggs
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt

## Steps

1. Slice the courgettes and onions thinly.
2. Soften the onion over low heat for about 10 minutes, then add the courgette.
3. Cook slowly, covered, until very tender (20 to 25 minutes).
4. Beat the eggs, stir them in and take it off the heat while still juicy.

## Note

The trick is patience: low heat, no rush.
